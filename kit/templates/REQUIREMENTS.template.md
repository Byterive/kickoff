# Requirements ledger

A requirement is added here when the user agrees it, in their words, and its status is checked against files or command output, never memory. Statuses: DONE, PARTIAL, MISSING, NOT BUILT, OPEN (needs the user's answer), THEIRS (someone else's action). Nothing is reported done without a line here that says where the evidence is.
